Current Listings

Three good places to start.

Mighty Dads does not run these groups. We are surfacing them so dads can find real connection faster.

We highlight groups that look active and useful. Mighty Dads is not officially affiliated unless noted.

Parker Service-minded

Dads of Parker

A Parker-based dad community focused on service, friendship, and showing up locally. Good fit for dads who want a stronger local network and a group with a clear community mission.

  • Best for dads in Parker or the south metro area
  • Community-first and mission-driven
  • Good choice if you want local involvement, not just another online group
Visit Group
Denver Metro Kids welcome

Mile High Dads

A Denver-area Meetup group built around real connection, with a mix of playground meetups, coffee hangs, hikes, and dads night out. Strong fit for dads who want a group that feels easy to join.

  • Good for dads who want both kid-friendly meetups and dad-only time
  • Denver-area focus with recurring events
  • Solid first stop if you want low-pressure connection
View on Meetup
Denver Dad nights out

Denver Dads Meetup Group

A Denver Meetup group for fathers who want to build friendships with other dads through simple social events like bowling, brewery hangs, hikes, and other easy ways to connect.

  • Best for dads who want to meet other dads in Denver
  • Social, casual, and built around adult conversation
  • Good fit if you want connection without a big commitment upfront
